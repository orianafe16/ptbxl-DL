\paragraph{Own blocks.} I designed (with Mar\'ia's review), ran and analysed the exploratory analysis,
the Dense network, the 1D CNN and the generative models (ECG-GPT and LSTM generators). For each of them I
proposed the ablations, checked the code and the results, wrote the block note that the reports are
built from, and read the related literature (task T16: dataset and CNN papers). The generative block
was chosen to serve two areas at once: it is the autoregressive generator of area 3 and the
self-supervised model transferred in area 2, so the whole project stays on one use case.

\paragraph{Shared work.} With Mar\'ia I decided the dataset and the plan, wrote the training loop, and
ran the learning-strategy experiments, the zero-shot experiment, the 12-lead against lead II ablation
and the final comparison. I reviewed her data pipeline, her evaluation module, her recurrent networks
and her Transformer, and she reviewed my blocks.

\paragraph{This report.} The analysis of my blocks, the critical discussion and the conclusions are
mine; the summaries of Mar\'ia's blocks are based on her block notes and attributed to her. Figures,
tables and numbers are shared by our four reports and generated from the run logs.
