\paragraph{What I owned.} Four blocks are mine: the exploratory analysis, the dense network, the 1D CNN
and the two ECG generators. In each of them I chose the experiments (Mar\'ia reviewed the plan), checked
every line of code against the explanation of the technique before running it, executed the smoke tests,
the runs and the reports, inspected each figure against its numbers, and wrote the block note on which
this report is based. I proposed that the generator should double as the self-supervised model of
area~2, so that areas 2 and 3 share one use case. I also handled the literature on the dataset and the
CNN (T16), set up the repository and the environment, and kept the decisions log.

\paragraph{Joint work and review.} Mar\'ia and I chose the dataset, wrote the plan and the training loop,
and ran the transfer, zero-shot, lead~II and final-comparison experiments together. I reviewed her data
pipeline, evaluation module, recurrent networks and Transformer; she reviewed my four blocks.

\paragraph{This report.} I wrote the analysis of my own blocks, the critical discussion and the
conclusions with the assistant and checked every sentence against the logs. Mar\'ia's blocks appear here
only as summaries of her block notes, marked as hers. All figures, tables and numbers are shared by our
four reports and are generated from the run logs.
